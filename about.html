My Story

A Nail Technician's Journey

My name is Dammy, and I am the founder of Damsel Nails. My journey in the nail industry began with a strong passion for beauty and precision. Over the past seven years, I have dedicated myself to mastering advanced nail techniques, perfecting clean workmanship, and delivering high-quality results for every client. I believe that nails are more than just a beauty service — they are a form of self-expression and confidence.

At Damsel Nails, my focus is on professionalism, hygiene, and long-lasting results. Every set is created with care, attention to detail, and a commitment to excellence. My goal is to provide a comfortable experience and exceptional service, ensuring that each client leaves feeling valued and beautifully transformed.
