Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Damsel Nails Studio ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information submitted through our website and other communication channels.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us, request information, or book an appointment, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number or WhatsApp contact details
  • Business or company name, when applicable
  • Messages or information you voluntarily provide
  • Appointment or service-related information

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your questions and enquiries
  • Process appointment or booking requests
  • Provide information about our services
  • Follow up with you regarding an enquiry
  • Improve our services and customer experience
  • Communicate with you when necessary regarding your request

3. Information From Social Media and Lead Forms

If you contact Damsel Nails Studio through platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, or other communication channels, we may receive information that you choose to provide through those platforms.

We use this information only for legitimate business purposes, such as responding to enquiries, providing requested information, and following up on service requests.

4. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information provided to us from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may use trusted third-party services and communication platforms to help us operate our business and respond to customer enquiries.

6. Third-Party Platforms

Our business may use third-party services such as Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Google services, email services, and automation tools.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these platforms when using their services.

7. Your Choices

You may contact us if you would like to ask about the personal information we hold about you or request that we correct or delete information where applicable.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact Damsel Nails Studio through the contact information provided on our website.
